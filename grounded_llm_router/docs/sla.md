# NimbusStack Service Level Agreement

## Uptime Commitment
NimbusStack commits to 99.9% uptime for the Growth and Enterprise tiers, measured monthly. The Starter tier does not carry an uptime SLA.

## Service Credits
If monthly uptime falls below 99.9% for eligible tiers, customers receive service credits applied to their next invoice:
- 99.0%–99.9% uptime: 10% credit
- 95.0%–99.0% uptime: 25% credit
- Below 95.0% uptime: 50% credit

Service credits must be requested within 30 days of the incident and are not issued automatically.

## Scheduled Maintenance
Scheduled maintenance windows are excluded from uptime calculations. NimbusStack provides at least 72 hours' notice for scheduled maintenance via email and the status page.

## Support Response Times
- Starter: 48-hour email response, business days only
- Growth: 12-hour email response, 7 days a week
- Enterprise: 1-hour response via dedicated support engineer, 24/7

## Support Response Times do not apply to feature requests, only to service-impacting issues (outages, data access problems, security concerns).
