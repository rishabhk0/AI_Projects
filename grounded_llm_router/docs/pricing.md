# NimbusStack Pricing

NimbusStack offers three pricing tiers, billed monthly or annually (annual billing gives a 15% discount).

## Starter — $29/month
- Up to 5 team members
- 100 GB storage
- Email support (48-hour response time)
- 1 project environment

## Growth — $99/month
- Up to 25 team members
- 1 TB storage
- Priority email support (12-hour response time)
- 5 project environments
- Basic API access (10,000 requests/day)

## Enterprise — Custom pricing
- Unlimited team members
- Unlimited storage
- Dedicated support engineer with 1-hour SLA response
- Unlimited project environments
- Full API access with custom rate limits
- Single Sign-On (SSO) and audit logging included

## Free Trial
All paid tiers include a 14-day free trial. No credit card is required to start a trial. Trials automatically expire after 14 days and do not auto-convert to a paid plan.

## Refund Policy
Monthly subscriptions can be cancelled at any time from the billing dashboard. Cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing cycle — we do not offer prorated refunds for partial months. Annual subscriptions can be refunded in full within the first 30 days of purchase.
